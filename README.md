# BigBasket Product Analysis Dashboard (Power BI)

An interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing BigBasket's product catalog
across pricing, ratings, brands, categories and discounts.

## Business Problem
Understand how product prices, ratings and discounts vary across
categories and brands to support better pricing and assortment decisions.

## Dataset
BigBasket product data (~24K products) with product, category,
sub-category, brand, sale price, market price and rating.
Source: [https://drive.google.com/file/d/11UQF9L0Q4DMclXuEk8Lt_q6eW8NjOXhX/view?usp=sharing]

## Key Metrics
- Total sale price: 8.12M
- Average sale price: 336.22
- Total products: ~24K
- Average rating: 3.99
- Average discount: 11.96%

## Dashboard Pages
1. **Overview** - KPIs, avg sale price by product and category, brand split
2. **Rating** - Avg rating per category, products by brand, rating vs sale price
3. **Product Analysis** - Detailed product table with market vs sale price
4. **Summary** - Products per brand, products by category, discount gauge

## Key Insights
- Kitchen, Garden & Pets and Baby Care have the highest average sale prices.
- Snacks & Branded Foods has the lowest average sale price.
- Eggs, Meat & Fish and Fruits & Vegetables have the highest average ratings.
- Kitchen, Garden & Pets has the lowest average rating.
- Average discount across products is about 12%.
